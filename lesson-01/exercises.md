# Lesson 1 Exercises

Complete all exercises independently.

## A. Conversation Comprehension

Answer in complete English sentences.

1. What is the man's name?
2. What is the woman's name?
3. Is Sarah a student?
4. Is Sarah a new student?
5. Where is Sarah from?
6. Where is Daniel from?
7. What does Sarah say after Daniel introduces himself?
8. What does Daniel say before they leave?

## B. Matching

Match each situation with the most appropriate expression.

1. It is 8:00 a.m.
2. It is 2:00 p.m.
3. It is 7:00 p.m.
4. You are meeting someone for the first time.
5. You are leaving someone and expect to see them later.
6. You are wishing someone a pleasant day.

Expressions:

a. Nice to meet you.
b. Good evening.
c. Good morning.
d. See you later.
e. Good afternoon.
f. Have a nice day!

## E. Situational Transfer

These questions are related to the lesson but do not simply repeat the conversation.

1. You meet a teacher at 8:00 a.m. What do you say?
2. You meet a classmate at 3:00 p.m. What do you say?
3. You meet someone at 7:00 p.m. What do you say?
4. Someone says, "Good morning." What do you say?
5. Someone says, "Nice to meet you." What do you say?
6. Someone asks, "How are you?" Give a natural answer.
7. Someone asks, "Where are you from?" Answer about yourself.
8. You are leaving a friend. Give a natural closing.

## G. Transformation

Change each sentence according to the instruction.

1. My name is Daniel. → Change the name to Sarah.
2. I'm from Indonesia. → Change the country to Malaysia.
3. Are you a student? → Change student to teacher.
4. I'm a new student. → Change student to teacher.
5. My name is Daniel. → Make a question asking for Daniel's name.
6. I'm from Malaysia. → Make a question asking about Daniel's origin.
7. Good morning. → Change the time to the evening.
8. I'm a new student. → Change new to old.

## I. Short Production

Write a short introduction about yourself.

Include:

- your name;
- whether you are a student;
- whether you are a new or old student;
- where you are from;
- a greeting;
- a closing expression.

Use your own information.
