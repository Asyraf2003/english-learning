# Lesson 1: Greetings and Introductions

## Conversation

**A:** Good morning. My name is Daniel. What's your name?

**B:** Good morning. I'm Sarah. Nice to meet you.

**A:** Nice to meet you, too. Are you a student here?

**B:** Yes, I am. I'm a new student.

**A:** Welcome! Where are you from?

**B:** I'm from Indonesia. How about you?

**A:** I'm from Malaysia. See you later.

**B:** See you. Have a nice day!
