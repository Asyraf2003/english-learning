# Kurikulum

The course begins at Lesson 1 / beginner level even when the student has prior exposure to English. Early lessons may move quickly when mastery is demonstrated.

## Progression

Foundation → everyday communication → broader real-world contexts → professional English → academic English → advanced English → IELTS preparation.

## Topic progression

Early chapters emphasize high-frequency daily situations such as greetings, introductions, family, food, home, school, routines, shopping, time, weather, and simple transport.

Later chapters gradually introduce markets, terminals, travel, restaurants, banks, campus/work contexts, offices, hospitals, administration, media, education, technology, society, economics, environment, and abstract discussion.

## Pacing

There is no mandatory one-chapter-per-day rule. A lesson can take one day, two days, three days, or longer. The teacher decides based on actual performance and cumulative load.

## Cumulative model

New lesson load stays reasonably controlled while the retrieval pool grows. Previous vocabulary, conversations, structures, and weaknesses remain eligible for future testing.

The course should create increasing cumulative difficulty without simply flooding the student with arbitrary new vocabulary.
