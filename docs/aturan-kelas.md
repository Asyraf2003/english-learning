# Aturan Kelas

- English is the default classroom language.
- The student independently searches meanings and prepares memorization.
- The teacher does not automatically translate or provide answer keys.
- New material is assigned according to mastery, not a rigid calendar.
- Previously learned material remains active in cumulative testing.
- Failed material returns to remedial review.
- Speaking and pronunciation are assessed through voice sessions.
- Assessment records are written only from actual evidence.
