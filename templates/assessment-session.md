# Assessment Session Template

Use this template for a dedicated assessment session.

## Before testing

Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/assessment.md`, the current lesson, and relevant prior lesson records. Identify recent material, weak items, failed items, and older material due for review.

## Test design

Assessment length is adaptive. Five or six good questions may be sufficient when the evidence is decisive. Expand the assessment only when performance is uncertain or more evidence is needed.

Possible areas:
- vocabulary recall;
- meaning recall;
- conversation recall;
- comprehension;
- new but related situations;
- sentence construction;
- grammar/pattern transformation;
- classroom/instructional English;
- speaking;
- pronunciation.

## Cumulative retrieval

As lessons accumulate, sample selected material from previous lessons rather than mechanically repeating everything. A later lesson may include several random questions from earlier conversations and several vocabulary items from older lessons.

Prioritize weak/failed material, recent material, and delayed-review material. Occasionally check strong/mastered material for retention.

## Memorization

Require active recall without looking. Possible checks include reproducing or continuing a conversation, answering questions, responding to situations, recalling vocabulary and meanings, and reconstructing sentence patterns.

Do not reveal answers before the attempt.

## Pass and remedial decision

Do not require perfection when the evidence is already sufficient. Record meaningful mistakes honestly. Move unstable material to remedial review and retest later.

Passing means the evidence is sufficient for progression, not that every possible question was answered correctly.

## Recording

After the assessment actually happens, update the lesson's `test-results.md` with observed results, score when appropriate, errors, conclusion, and remedial requirements. Update `conclusion.md` when the assessment establishes a meaningful lesson state or progression decision.

Never fabricate assessment evidence.
