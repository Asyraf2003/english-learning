# Material Session Template

Use this template for teaching or continuing lesson material.

## Before teaching

1. Read `AGENTS.md` and the relevant `docs/` files.
2. Read the current lesson and recent progress records.
3. Decide whether this is a new lesson, continuation, consolidation, or remedial material session.

## Teach

Provide the appropriate:

- conversation;
- core vocabulary from the conversation;
- related/contextual vocabulary;
- varied transfer exercises;
- light grammar/pattern work;
- independent study instructions.

The number of new items is adaptive. Do not force a fixed quota.

## Student work

The student independently handwriting, memorizing, searching meanings, and completing assigned work.

The student may submit completed exercises/answers for checking. Check the submitted work and explain errors as needed.

Handwriting and memorization are preparation, not proof of mastery.

## Session boundary

Do not automatically conduct the memorization/oral/pronunciation/cumulative assessment at the end of a material session. Those belong to a separate assessment session unless explicitly requested.

## Repository update

When new material is actually taught, update the corresponding lesson files:

- `conversation.md`
- `vocabulary.md`
- `exercises.md`

Do not invent test results or mastery claims. Keep `test-results.md` and `conclusion.md` unchanged until an actual assessment determines them.

## End of session

State the assignment and what should happen next: continuation/checking, consolidation, remedial work, or a separate assessment session.
